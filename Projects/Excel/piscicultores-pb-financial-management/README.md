# Piscicultores PB Financial Management System

A financial management and operational tracking workbook designed for a family-owned aquaculture business located in Piedra Blanca, Monseñor Nouel, Dominican Republic.

The system provides complete visibility into:

- Capital contributions from partners
- USD to DOP currency conversion tracking
- Accounts payable management
- Operating expenses
- Employee payments
- Transportation and travel expenses
- Aquaculture production investments
- Revenue tracking
- Cash flow monitoring
- Financial dashboards and KPI reporting

## Business Context

Piscicultores PB is a tilapia farming operation funded by three partners through international remittances. Funds are converted from USD to Dominican Pesos (DOP) and used to support farm development, operations, infrastructure, labor, and fish production activities.

## Key Features

- Partner Capital Tracking
- Multi-Currency Accounting (USD/DOP)
- Accounts Payable Management
- Expense Categorization
- Bank Reconciliation
- Revenue Tracking
- Cash Flow Monitoring
- Executive Dashboard
- Aquaculture Production Tracking
